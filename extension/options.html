    Answer on the new tab page

    With your own OpenAI Platform key, Archer can stream an answer straight onto the new tab instead of sending you somewhere. Everything else keeps working exactly as it does now.

    Your key stays on this device. It is kept in this browser's extension storage and sent only to api.openai.com, directly from the new tab page. There is no Archer server, nothing is proxied, and nothing is logged anywhere else. You are billed by OpenAI for what you use, on your own account.

    A Platform key from platform.openai.com — not your ChatGPT login. The two are separate accounts, and a Platform key cannot see your ChatGPT conversations.

    Model

    Listed from your own key, so this is what your account can actually reach rather than a list baked into the extension.

    Budget

    Counted in tokens, which the API reports exactly. A dollar figure needs a price list, and a price list hardcoded into an extension goes stale silently and in the wrong direction — so Archer only shows one if you enter your own rates.
